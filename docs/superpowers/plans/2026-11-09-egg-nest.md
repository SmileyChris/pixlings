# Egg Nest Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** From the second hatch on, a mature pixling lays a nest of two eggs, plus a shiny third when it has laid one. Each egg has a seeded look, a rarity mark and a partner. The picked egg hatches with a blended family name. Release below level 5 is refused.

**Architecture:** Four new pure modules hold the rules. `names.ts` blends names, `eggs.ts` holds egg looks and their 16×16 pixels, `nest.ts` works out a nest from the parent and hatches the pick, and `progress.ts` stores the line and the family history. `register.tsx` adds the release gate and the nest flow, and the room (`room.ts`) draws the nest. The hatch animation draws the picked egg.

**Tech Stack:** TypeScript run as-is by Node 24 and the Claude Code plugin runtime. Tests use `claude-code/testing` via `claude plugin test plugins/pixlings`.

**Spec:** `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-08-egg-nest-design.md`. The drawing reference is `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-11-08-egg-nest-mockup.html` (`makeEgg`, `paint`, `shine`, `leanToGold`, `blend`).

## Global Constraints

- `MATURE_LEVEL` stays 5. Below it, `/pixling hatch` replies exactly `${name} hasn't matured yet (level ${level} of 5). Keep going together a little longer before hatching a new egg.` and nothing changes.
- The first egg ever hatches by itself, as today (`hatchPixling(Math.random, now())`). It gets no nest and no look: the cream egg. Its name is plain from `NAMES`.
- Rarity weights, species art, the level curve and `shinyEggOf` don't change.
- The nest has no ranking text anywhere: no rarity words, stars, group names or "shiny". After the hatch, the toast and card are as they are today.
- Patterns: Speckled, Spotted, Dappled, Marbled, Dusted. Shells: Cream, Rose, Sand, Fog, Peach, Charcoal, with the `W/S/L/K/M` values from the mockup's `PALETTES`. Marks: common = none; uncommon = Mossy (`MOSS 0x6fdc8c`, `MOSS_DARK 0x3f9e5c`, `LEAF 0xa8f0bd`); rare = Glowing (`GLOW 0x5aa9ff`, `GLOW_HOT 0xd6ebff`, 4 veins); epic and legendary = Gilded (`GOLD 0xffc53d`, `GOLD_SHADE 0xc98f1a`, a cap on rows 2–5, 7 flecks, the outline leaning toward gold by 0.65 × {1 on rows ≤4, 0.65 on row 5, 0.3 on row 6} while keeping its own HSL lightness).
- The egg shine is stronger than a pixling's: a beat every 1500 ms, a sheen on 45% of beats, a 0.38 lift, a sheen of 650 ms, and a flare of 1–2 on every beat. The pixling shine (`SHINE_MS 4500`, 0.15, 0.1) doesn't change.
- Blending: start = `/^[^aeiouy]*[aeiouy]+/`, end = `/[^aeiouy]*[aeiouy]+[^aeiouy]*$/`, both on lower case. When the start ends in a vowel and the end begins with one, drop the end's leading vowels. Capitalise the result. `y` counts as a vowel.
- Partners are decoration only. The nest shows `♥`, then a tiny partner picture on Svg surfaces only, then the partner's name. On the terminal's Raster it's `♥ Tango`.
- Copy: the nest heading is `${name}'s eggs`. The note is `Pick one to hatch. ${name} will be released: level and stats start over; your badges, streak, tics and dex are kept.` The keep button reads `Keep ${name}`. The card line is `Child of ${parent}.`

## Review Focus

1. **Two terminals, one nest:** if one picks while the other still shows the nest, a pick in the second does nothing, and its nest closes once it sees the new pixling. Test in Task 5.
2. **Names that aren't plain words:** a renamed or adopted name like `Sir Fluff`, `R2-D2`, `Ωmega` or `X` blends into something non-empty of at most 24 characters, without throwing. Test in Task 1.
3. **An old save:** a save with `eggSeed`, no `line` and no `family` loads, shows no parent, and can lay a nest at level 5. Test in Task 2.
4. **A shiny egg laid while a nest waits:** the two base eggs stay identical and a third appears. Test in Task 4.
5. **A session restarting mid-hatch:** a reload inside the hatch window draws the picked egg's look, not the cream egg. A save without `egg` falls back to the cream egg without throwing. Test in Task 3.

### Task 3: Egg looks

**Files:**
- Create: `plugins/pixlings/hooks/lib/eggs.ts`
- Modify: `plugins/pixlings/hooks/lib/canvas.ts:1062-1140` (`EGG` and `renderHatch`)
- Test: `plugins/pixlings/tests/eggs.test.ts` (create)

**Interfaces:**
- Produces:
  - `type EggLook = { seed: number; pattern: 'speckled' | 'spotted' | 'dappled' | 'marbled' | 'dusted'; shell: 'cream' | 'rose' | 'sand' | 'fog' | 'peach' | 'charcoal'; mark: 'plain' | 'mossy' | 'glowing' | 'gilded'; isShiny: boolean; … }`. The extra fields are the seeded spots, veins, flecks, phase and clock offset.
  - `eggLookOf(seed: number, rarity: Rarity, isShiny: boolean): EggLook`.
  - `eggPixels(look: EggLook | null, t: number): (number | null)[]`: a 16×16 buffer, row-major, with null for empty. A null look gives today's cream egg.
  - `EGG_SHAPE: readonly string[]`: the 16 rows, moved from `canvas.ts`'s `EGG`.
  - `describeEgg(look: EggLook): string`: alt text, for example `'A speckled rose egg with a green vine'`. The marks read `'with a green vine'`, `'with glowing blue veins'` and `'with a gold cap'`, plus `', shimmering'` when shiny. Plain has no suffix.
  - `renderHatch(input: FrameInput, rarityColor: number, look: EggLook | null = null)`. It stamps `eggPixels(look, t)` where it stamps `EGG` today. The cracks and the light keep today's colours and positions.
- `eggs.ts` imports nothing from `canvas.ts`, which avoids an import cycle, so it keeps its own `mix`. The shiny shine on an egg uses the strong constants from the Global Constraints over the egg's own outline, ported from the mockup's `shine`.

- [ ] **Step 1: Write the failing tests** in `tests/eggs.test.ts`:
  - `a seed always gives the same look`: `eggLookOf(42,'rare',false)` deep-equals itself on a second call, and `eggPixels` at t=0 is equal too.
  - `all 30 looks turn up`: over 2,000 seeds, every pattern × shell pair appears.
  - `the mark follows the rarity`: common is plain, uncommon mossy, rare glowing, and both epic and legendary gilded. `eggLookOf(s,'epic',false)` and `eggLookOf(s,'legendary',false)` have the same `eggPixels` at t=0.
  - `marks stay inside the shell`: for every mark and shiny state at t ∈ {0, 700, 1600}, each pixel that isn't null lies where `EGG_SHAPE` isn't `'.'`. A strong shine's flare may light one pixel next to the outline, so allow the outline's 4-neighbours when shiny.
  - `the gilded outline keeps its lightness`: for each shell, the outline pixels on rows ≤4 have the same HSL lightness as the shell's `K` (±0.01) and a hue in [30°, 50°].
  - `plain eggs hold still, shiny and glowing ones move`: a plain egg that isn't shiny gives the same pixels at t=0 and t=900. A shiny egg's pixels differ between some pair of `t` in `0..6000` in 100 ms steps, and so do a glowing egg's.
  - `the hatch draws the picked egg`: `renderHatch(input, color, look)` at t=200 differs from `renderHatch(input, color)` for a rose gilded look. With look null, it matches today's frame, the cream egg, pixel for pixel.

### Task 4: The nest

**Files:**
- Create: `plugins/pixlings/hooks/lib/nest.ts`
- Modify: `plugins/pixlings/hooks/lib/progress.ts:146` (split `roll`)
- Test: `plugins/pixlings/tests/nest.test.ts`

**Interfaces:**
- Consumes: `blend` and `freshName` (Task 1); `Line`, `Ancestor`, `usedNames` and `ancestorOf` (Task 2); `eggLookOf` (Task 3); `shinyEggOf` and `seededRandom`.
- Produces:
  - `rollSpecies(random: () => number): Species` in `progress.ts`. `roll` now calls it, then picks a name. The order of calls on `random` stays the same, so the first eggs of today's seeds don't change.
  - `type Egg = { seed: number; species: Species; name: string; partner: string; isShiny: boolean; look: EggLook }`.
  - `nestOf(parent: Pixling): Egg[]`: two eggs, plus a third with `isShiny: true` when `shinyEggOf(parent)`.
  - `partnerSpecies(name: string): Species`: `rollSpecies(seededRandom(hash of name.toLowerCase()))`.
  - `hatchEgg(parent: Pixling, egg: Egg, now: number): Pixling`.
- How it's worked out: `r = seededRandom(parent.hatchedAt)` gives the seeds `[⌊r()·2³¹⌋, ⌊r()·2³¹⌋, ⌊r()·2³¹⌋]`, with egg *i* using seed *i*. The shiny egg is always the third seed. Each egg is worked out from `e = seededRandom(seed)`:
  - species `rollSpecies(e)`;
  - partner `freshName(e, usedNames(parent))`;
  - name: when `parent.line.side` is `'start'`, `blend(parent.line.newer, partner)`, otherwise `blend(partner, parent.line.newer)`;
  - look `eggLookOf(seed, species.rarity, isShiny)`.
- `hatchEgg` returns the following, with `stats.sessions` set to 1 as `nextEgg` did:
  - `hatchPixling`'s fresh fields;
  - `species`, `name` and `egg: egg.seed` from the egg;
  - `isShiny: egg.isShiny`;
  - `line: { newer: egg.partner, side: the other side, isStart: false }`;
  - `family: [...parent.family, ancestorOf(parent, now)]`;
  - `dex` plus `egg.species.id` only;
  - `badges`, `streak` and `tics` carried over;
  - `lifetime.shinyEggs` going up by 1 when `shinyEggOf(parent)`, whichever egg is picked.
