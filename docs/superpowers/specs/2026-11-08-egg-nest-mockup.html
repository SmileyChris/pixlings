Pixling Egg Looks

At a hatch, two eggs are laid and you keep one. Each egg's pattern and colour come from its seed and a mark on top shows its rarity group. A shiny egg comes as a third egg, and only its shine gives it away. Click an egg to hatch it.

A shiny egg comes as a third egg, told apart only by its shine. The parent is Miffles (Miso + Waffles), so this hatch keeps Waffles on the end and rolls a new start.

Family line

Pick eggs one after another, with no hatching, to watch a line grow. Each egg shows its other parent; its own name stays hidden until you pick it. The newest name in each pixling carries on, on the same side, and a new roll fills the other half. Click a faded egg in an earlier generation to go back and take that branch instead.

    The four groups

    The groups are named for how they look, not ranked. Epic and legendary share Gilded. The light through the cracks gives a legendary away at the last moment: gold instead of purple.

    Every look

    Five natural patterns, the kind real eggs have, and six shell colours make 30 base looks. Dusted is Crowned turned upside down: its speckles settle toward the base, leaving the top clear for a gold cap. The spots, speckles, blotches, veins and flecks all move with the seed, so two eggs with the same look still differ.

    Click a row to step its eggs through the shell colours.
